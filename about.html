Why Little Treasures?


We welcome you to Little Treasures World; a haven created with love and intention for parents who desire to raise children grounded in faith, wisdom, and purpose. We understand that parenting is one of life’s greatest callings — a divine assignment entrusted to us by God — and we are here to walk alongside you on this sacred journey. This is more than just a resource hub; it is a community where parents can find encouragement, inspiration, and practical tools to navigate the beautiful yet sometimes challenging task of raising children.


At the heart of what we do is a deep belief: it is a sacred honor to birth, nurture, and shape the lives of the little treasures God has entrusted to us. Parenting is intentional, and we are called to do it in love, discipline, and obedience to God’s word. Our mission is to equip parents with the tools and guidance they need to lead their homes with purpose, plant seeds of faith, and raise Kingdom-minded children who will shine their light in the world. We believe one of the greatest gifts we can offer our children is consistent exposure to God’s Word — not just in church but within our homes, woven into the rhythm of our daily lives. As we teach, model, and live out God’s principles, we give our children a firm foundation that will guide them for a lifetime. Together, let us build a generation of confident, compassionate, and Christ-centered children — children who know who they are, whose they are, and who boldly walk in their God-given purpose.


Mrs. J.A.A. Unknown

President, Little Treasures World.

Model of Operations & Values

Our value system and core operational guide is dedicated to strengthening parental skills and supporting healthy child development. Our structured programs include; practical parenting workshops, guidance counseling, and educational resources that empower parents to build strong family relationships. Also, spanning across the trainings on positive discipline, effective communication, emotional intelligence, and child safety. Through community outreach and support groups, our programs create safe spaces for parents to learn and share experiences. Furthermore, our programs also promote early childhood education and developmental monitoring to ensure children reach key milestones. Our programs encourage nurturing environments that foster confidence, academic growth, and emotional well-being, helping parents become active partners in their children’s holistic development.
