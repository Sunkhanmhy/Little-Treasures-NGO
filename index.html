About Little Treasures


Welcome to Little Treasures World ; a haven created with love and intention for parents who desire to raise children grounded in faith, wisdom, and purpose. We understand that parenting is one of life’s greatest callings — a divine assignment entrusted to us by God — and we are here to walk alongside you on this sacred journey.


This is more than just a resource hub; it is a community where parents can find encouragement, inspiration, and practical tools to navigate the beautiful yet sometimes challenging task of raising children.

Our Mission & Vision


Our mission and vision is to empower parents and children through access to quality education, nurturing their potential to become confident, capable, and compassionate individuals. We believe that every child, regardless of their background or circumstance, deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, and thrive.


Education is not just a pathway to knowledge, it is a bridge to hope, equality, and lasting change. We aim to break the cycle of poverty and unhealthy parental system in the society by providing resources, mentorship, and holistic support that address both academic and emotional needs.

Our Programs & Initiatives

 

The Tomike Initiative


Why Tomike Initiatives? This initiative is designed to serve as a beacon of hope and support for widows, the elderly, and vulnerable women within our community. It goes beyond meeting physical needs — it seeks to nurture the spirit, soul, and body. Our vision is to create a warm and welcoming space where women can gather for fellowship, encouragement, and empowerment. The initiative focuses on: Conducting basic health check-ups and general wellness assessments, providing food & grocery supplies.
